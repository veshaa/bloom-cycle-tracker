Period 101

Understand the basic stages of the menstrual cycle.

Understanding the Menstrual Cycle

The menstrual cycle prepares the body for a possible pregnancy. Length varies person to person — this is normal.


The 4 Main Phases:
  • 🩸 Menstrual Phase — Lining sheds; bleeding typically 3–7 days.
  • 🌱 Follicular Phase — Body prepares an egg; energy may rise.
  • 💜 Ovulation Phase — Egg released; most fertile time.
  • 🌙 Luteal Phase — Prepares for next period; PMS may appear.
🌿

Period Cramps

Common discomfort & how to feel better.

Period Cramps — What Helps?

Caused by prostaglandins making the uterus contract.


✅ Things That May Help:
  • 🔥 Heat — hot water bottle or warm towel
  • 🚶 Gentle movement — walking or stretching
  • 💧 Hydration — reduces water retention
  • 🍵 Ginger or chamomile tea

⚠️ See a doctor if pain stops daily activities.
